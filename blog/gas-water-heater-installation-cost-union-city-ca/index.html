What You Are Really Paying For

Ask five plumbers what it costs to install a gas water heater and you will hear five different answers. The gap is rarely greed. The quotes are usually describing different jobs. One bid includes a new gas shutoff and venting corrections, while another assumes the old connections are fine.

This guide shows how a gas water heater installation quote is built, so you can compare bids fairly. We cover the tank and the labor, then the code items that tend to vanish from low bids. If you are still deciding whether to fix or replace, our page on water heater replacement in Union City covers that call.

Five Things That Move a Quote Up or Down

Tank size and efficiencyA 40-gallon and a 50-gallon tank are different products, and a higher-efficiency model costs more at the supply house than a basic one. Size the tank to your household's busiest morning, not to whatever the old tank happened to be.
Where the heater sitsA garage unit on a flat slab is a quick swap. An attic or tight closet unit needs a drain pan, a drain line, and carrying a full-weight tank through a hatch. Access changes labor hours more than almost anything else.
Venting conditionA gas heater sends combustion gases up a flue. If the old flue is corroded, undersized or pitched wrong, it must be corrected, and that should be its own line item, separate from the heater.
Gas and water connectionsFlexible gas connectors, a gas shutoff that closes fully, a sediment trap and sound water connectors all get checked on a proper install. Corroded fittings turn a simple swap into a repair job.
Code items and disposalSeismic strapping, a drain pan where required, a discharge pipe on the relief valve, an expansion tank on closed systems, the permit and hauling away the old tank each show up as lines. Skipping them lowers the quote and raises your risk.
No honest article can print a number here, because the right price depends on your house. A written quote that itemizes these five areas is the only figure worth trusting.

Union City Details That Belong on Your Quote

Water from the Alameda County Water District in Union City is a blend that ACWD reports at 120 to 160 ppm hardness, averaging 138 ppm, or about 8 grains per gallon. That is moderately hard, so scale slowly builds at the bottom of a tank. Ask for a drain valve you can reach and plan a periodic flush to protect the burner and tank floor.

California requires new and replacement water heaters to be braced, anchored or strapped against earthquake motion (Health and Safety Code 19211). The Hayward Fault produced the 1868 magnitude 6.8 earthquake, with rupture traced from Warm Springs in Fremont north to San Leandro. If strapping is missing from your quote, ask why.

Union City's Building Division says a plumbing and mechanical permit is needed for most construction, but its documents do not name water heaters. Call the Building Division at 510.675.5313 and ask whether your swap needs one. Then ask your plumber who handles it.

Questions to Ask Before You Accept a Bid

  • ✓Is this a like-for-like swap, or does the gas line, vent or location change?
  • ✓Will you reuse my old flue, and have you looked at it?
  • ✓Is the expansion tank, strapping and relief valve discharge pipe included?
  • ✓Who pulls the permit, and who is home for the inspection?
  • ✓What is the tank size and recovery rating, and why that size for my household?
  • ✓Is disposal of the old unit included?
  • ✓What happens if you open the wall or flue and find a problem?
  • ✓What does the written warranty cover, in writing, and who honors it?

Write the answers on the quote itself. A plumber who is comfortable with a complete job will not mind.
