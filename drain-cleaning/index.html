Sewage or Wastewater Coming Back Up? Start Here

A drain that is overflowing, or a toilet that bubbles while the shower runs, cannot wait for morning. Call the 24/7 line and we will help you sort out the next step. While you wait, stop using water, keep sinks, tubs and the washer off, and move rugs and stored boxes away from low floor drains.

If water is rising in the tub or shower when you flush, the blockage is almost always downstream of every fixture, in the main line. If only one sink is slow, the problem is local to that branch. That one observation shortens the diagnosis a great deal, so tell us what you saw and in what order.

Do not pour drain cleaner into a standing, backed-up line. It sits in the pipe, does not reach the blockage, and puts caustic liquid where we have to work with our hands and tools. Same-day and after-hours calls go through the same line, so ask about timing when you call.

By fixture

Kitchen, Bathroom, Main Line and Floor Drain Clogs

Kitchen sink and dishwasher drain

Cooking fat cools and coats the pipe wall, and food scraps catch on it. A kitchen sink that clears briefly and then slows again is usually coated, not blocked. If the disposal hums but water will not leave, see garbage disposal repair before running it more.

Bathroom sink, tub and shower

Hair and soap scum build a mat around the stopper and in the trap. These are the easiest clogs to reach and the ones a plastic hair tool does well on. If the tub is slow and the sink is fine, the clog sits in that fixture's own branch.

Main line and laundry

A main drain carries everything the house sends out, so a clog here backs up the lowest fixture first, often a shower, tub or the washer standpipe. We run a larger drain machine through the cleanout or a vent, and follow with a camera when the clog is not a one-time event.

Floor drains and yard drains

Garage, laundry room and basement-style floor drains collect dirt and lint that never meet a fixture trap. Yard drains pick up soil and roots. Both respond to a drain machine, and stubborn ones need hydro jetting to wash the line out.

What We Do and What You Can Safely Try First

Safe to try yourself

  • Plunge a single clogged sink or tub with a proper cup plunger and the overflow blocked
  • Pull the stopper and clear the hair from it by hand
  • Take apart and clean an under-sink P-trap with a bucket below
  • Run hot tap water, not boiling, down a greasy but still moving kitchen drain
  • Stop all water use the moment a second fixture backs up

Leave it to us

  • Any clog that affects more than one fixture
  • Sewage coming up in a tub, shower or floor drain
  • A cable or auger that stops hard and will not advance
  • A clog that has cleared and come back more than once
  • Anything that needs a drain machine, hydro jetter or camera

Chemical openers and rented augers are where most home attempts go wrong. Acid and lye products can damage older pipe and leave a hazard for whoever opens the trap next. A rented cable can wedge in a bend or tear a joint, which turns a clog into a repair.

Drain Snaking, Machine Work and When Jetting Is Better

Snaking means feeding a rotating cable into the pipe until it catches the clog, then pulling or breaking it apart. We match the cable and cutting head to the pipe, a small cable for a bathroom branch and a heavier machine for a main line. For a simple hair or paper clog, this is the right and quickest tool.

Snaking punches a hole through a clog. It does not always scrape the whole pipe wall, so grease and scale can remain and rebuild. When a line is coated rather than plugged, we recommend jetting, which scours the full diameter with a high-pressure stream.

If the clog returns, the pipe, not the debris, is the real problem. A sag, a root entry or a broken section catches whatever comes through, no matter how often it is cleared. That is when we pull a camera through, or schedule a planned sewer inspection so you can see it for yourself.

Whose Pipe Is It? Sewer Basics in Union City

Union Sanitary District (USD) runs the public sewer main for Union City, Fremont and Newark, and the property owner is responsible for the whole lateral from the house to the main. If sewage is backing up, USD takes calls 24 hours a day at (510) 477-7500 and checks its own main at no charge.

That call helps you decide who to hire. If the main is clear, the blockage is probably in your lateral, which is yours to clear, and we can come out to do it. We also serve Fremont and the Tri-City area, see emergency plumbing in Fremont.

Drain and vent layout follow the California Plumbing Code, which is based on the Uniform Plumbing Code (UPC), which is why a drain that keeps slowing after cleaning can point to slope or venting, not just buildup. Ask the plumber to tell you if the camera or the machine suggests a layout problem.

While you wait

Quick Checks Before the Plumber Arrives

  • ✓Note the pattern. Write down which fixtures are slow, which gurgle and which back up. Order matters.
  • ✓Stop the water. No laundry, dishwasher, showers or flushing until the line is open.
  • ✓Find the cleanout. A capped pipe near the house or behind the sidewalk gives us direct access to the main line.
  • ✓Clear the floor. Move rugs, boxes and electronics away from any drain that might overflow.
  • ✓Keep pets and kids away. Wastewater carries bacteria, so wear gloves and wash after cleanup.
  • ✓Skip the chemicals. Tell us if you already poured any, so we handle the line safely.
