A faucet that will not shut off at 2 a.m.

Faucets fail at awkward hours because they run all day and nobody notices until the house is quiet. A stream that continues with the handle off means the cartridge or valve seat inside is worn through. A stream that blasts from the base or the supply line means a hose or fitting has failed.

Start under the sink. Each faucet should have its own quarter-turn or multi-turn stop valve on the hot and cold lines, and turning them off will stop most spraying. If the valve does not hold, or the faucet is on a laundry sink or a hose bib, shut off the main house valve and call.

Do not keep cranking the handle to stop a drip. That grinds the seat and turns a small repair into a valve replacement.

What is inside

Cartridge, ball, disc or washer: how the faucet is built

Four designs cover most faucets in Union City homes. Knowing which you own tells you what the fix really involves.

Cartridge faucets

Single-lever kitchen and bath faucets use a plug-in cartridge. When the lever feels gritty or the water cannot be shut off, a new cartridge restores a smooth stop. Parts must match the brand and model.

Ball faucets

Found on many older kitchen faucets, with a rotating ball and rubber seats. Drips from the spout or leaks around the handle usually mean worn seats and springs, which come in a service kit.

Ceramic disc faucets

Two polished discs slide against each other, and they are long-lived. When one drips, scale or sediment on the discs is the usual culprit, so the cartridge is cleaned or swapped.

Compression faucets

Two handles, each with a rubber washer pressing on a seat. They drip when the washer hardens or the seat pits. A pitted seat can be resurfaced or the stem replaced.

What Union City water does to faucets

ACWD reports that the blended supply reaching Union City runs 120 to 160 ppm hardness (average 138, about 8 grains per gallon), and it is treated with chloramines. Hard water leaves scale on aerators and disc faces, and chloramines are commonly blamed for stiffening rubber washers and seals over the years.

That is why a faucet that slowly loses flow often needs its aerator soaked in vinegar and its cartridge cleaned, not replaced. If you are shopping for a new faucet, note that the 2025 California code limits kitchen faucets to 1.8 gpm and bathroom faucets to 1.2 gpm, so a new bath faucet may feel softer than the old one.

Below the sink

Sink drains and traps: why the water will not leave

Many faucet calls turn out to be drain calls. A sink that fills and drains slowly is rarely the faucet's fault.

  1. Check the stopper and strainer

    Bathroom pop-up stoppers collect hair, and kitchen strainer baskets collect scraps. Removing and cleaning them solves a surprising share of slow sinks.

  2. Open the P-trap

    The curved pipe under the sink holds a water seal against sewer gas and catches debris. We loosen the slip nuts over a bucket, clear it, and reseat the washers.

  3. Snake the branch drain

    If the trap is clean and the sink still crawls, the blockage is in the wall pipe. A small drain machine reaches it without damaging the fixture.

  4. Replace worn parts

    Brittle trap arms, corroded tailpieces and leaking washers get swapped for new fittings.

If every sink in the house is slow, jump to sewer line cleaning because the blockage is farther down the line.

Faucet brand and styleParts for common brands are easy to source. Specialty finishes or imported designs take longer to match.
Valve conditionStiff or leaking stop valves add work, because they are better replaced than reused.

Decision

Repair the faucet or buy a new one

Repair when

  • The body is sound with no pitting or cracks
  • A cartridge or washer kit is readily available
  • The finish is in good shape
  • The leak is at a stem, hose or supply line

Replace when

  • The body is corroded or the finish is peeling
  • Parts are discontinued or the faucet is a decades-old design
  • The faucet has been repaired several times already
  • You want a lower-flow or touchless model

Outside the house, a water line repair may be the real answer if the leak is on the supply side of the hose bib.
