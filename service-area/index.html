Union City neighborhoods and what each one needs

Union City formed in 1959 from Alvarado, Decoto and New Haven. The modern map has more pieces, but these are the names callers use.

Alvarado

Alvarado has a historic district and a California Historical Landmark marking the site of Alameda County's first courthouse at 30977 Union City Blvd. On older streets, ask the plumber to run a camera through the sewer before replacing fixtures, since age often shows up underground.

Decoto

Decoto was one of the communities that merged into Union City. If your home was built before 1960, ACWD says a galvanized service line is likely, so a pressure drop deserves a look at the line from the meter.

Union Landing area

Our address is here, near the theaters, restaurants and Sugar Mill Landing Park with its Flight 93 Memorial at Smith and Dyer Streets. Restaurant owners should ask about grease interceptors and floor drains, covered on the commercial plumbing page.

Station District and BART

The Station District is the transit-oriented area around Union City BART at 10 Union Square. In apartment and other multi-unit buildings, a leak may involve a shared shutoff, so find out whether the building has a master valve before calling.

Mission Blvd corridor

Mission Blvd runs toward Fremont and passes the Masonic Home at 34400 Mission Blvd. Properties on or near it use the same ACWD water and USD sewer as the rest of the city.

Who owns the problem? A short walk-through

  1. Find the water meter

    ACWD maintains pipe on the street side of the meter. If the leak is upstream of it, call ACWD Operations at (510) 668-6500.

  2. Look at the meter side

    The owner maintains everything from the meter into the property. This includes the water line to the house, which is where our water line repair page picks up.

  3. Check the sewer cleanout

    Union Sanitary District says the owner maintains the full lateral to the main. A cleanout outside the house or just behind the sidewalk is the usual access point, and older homes may not have one.

  4. Ask USD to check the main

    USD's 24 hour line is (510) 477-7500, and it inspects the public main for free. If the main is clear, the problem is on your side and you need a plumber.

Hayward Fault and Alameda Creek: two local facts that affect plumbing

The Union City Station District sits west of the Hayward Fault, with the nearest trace about 680 feet to the northeast, according to the city's environmental review. California requires water heaters to be braced or strapped against earthquake movement, so a heater with no straps is both a code issue and a risk for a gas leak. After any strong shake, check for water and gas leaks and shut off both at the main if you find one.

The Alameda Creek Flood Control Channel runs through Union City and Fremont, and the Alameda Creek Regional Trail has entrances here. Water showing up in a yard or crawl space after rain may be drainage rather than plumbing, so check roof gutters and grading before assuming a pipe has broken.

Source type: City of Union City environmental review, California Seismic Safety Commission, Alameda County Flood Control District

Hayward, Newark and Castro Valley: what to expect

We take calls from Hayward, Newark and Castro Valley as well as Union City and Fremont. Distances are rough estimates by road. Central Fremont is about 4 to 5 miles from Union City, Newark's city hall is about the same, and downtown Hayward is about 8 miles. Castro Valley lies a couple of miles north of the city line, past the Fairview area.

In Hayward and Castro Valley the main line owner is a different agency, so a sewer backup goes to the city of Hayward or the Castro Valley Sanitary District rather than USD. Our work on the private side of the meter and lateral is the same. For a quick start, begin with drain cleaning or leak detection, depending on what you see.
