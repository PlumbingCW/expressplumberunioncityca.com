How to choose the right service

If you are not sure what to call it, describe what you see and let the dispatcher sort it out. Still, a little sorting yourself saves time. One slow drain is a drain cleaning job. Every drain slow at once is the main line, which means sewer line cleaning or a camera look.

Water that is hot for a moment and then cold usually points to a failing part, and water heater repair is often cheaper than starting over. A heater that leaks from the tank body is finished, and water heater replacement is the right call. A water bill that jumped with no change in habits is a leak detection question.

Planned work follows a similar logic. A new bathroom, a moved sink or a kitchen remodel belongs under plumbing installation. Repeated pinhole leaks in copper or galvanized lines are the point to ask about repiping rather than patching one spot at a time.

For anything urgent, remember the order of ownership. The Alameda County Water District maintains the pipe on the street side of the meter, you maintain everything from the meter in, and Union Sanitary District owns the public sewer main while you own the lateral. We can tell you which side a problem sits on during the call.

Which code applies

Every job on this list follows the California Plumbing Code, which is based on the Uniform Plumbing Code (UPC), so any plumber you hire should be able to say which section applies to your work. That question is a quick way to separate careful contractors from careless ones.
