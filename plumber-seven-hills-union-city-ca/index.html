The area

Where Seven Hills is

Union City's long range planning page names the Seven Hills neighborhood and describes a Hillside Specific Plan that guides homes on the land above it. A local park review places Seven Hills Park on Florence Street, up in the hills off Mission Blvd. A 2020 city environmental document puts Florence Street about 300 feet east of Riviera Drive.

That same 2020 document described a proposed six-lot custom-home subdivision on a hillside property at the south end of Florence Street. We could not confirm whether it was built, so treat it as a proposal. The city's environmental review of the Station District also notes that the foothills of the Coastal Range frame the eastern edge of the city.

Listing sites describe mostly single-family homes in Seven Hills, with build years they give between 1965 and 1984. We treat that as a rough guide and not a fact about your house. Water service is from ACWD and sewer service from Union Sanitary District, the same as the rest of the city.

Union Sanitary District

Sewers on a slope

USD's own guidance says a backflow prevention device on the sewer may be prudent in hilly areas, and that USD does not supply it. A backwater valve sits in the lateral and lets sewage leave the house but stops it from coming back. Whether you need one depends on where your lowest fixture sits compared with the nearest manhole.

That is a question to ask a plumber who can see your lateral. A camera inspection shows the pipe and where a cleanout is, and the answer is not the same for every lot. Any lateral work needs a USD repair permit, even on private property.

Remember who owns what. The district owns the public main, and the owner owns the whole lateral from the house to that main. When sewage backs up, call USD at (510) 477-7500 first so it can check its main, then call us about the lateral if the main is clear. Our drain cleaning page covers the clearing step.

General information

Pressure, hills and your meter

Water pressure rises and falls with elevation. We could not find an ACWD pressure figure for Seven Hills, so we do not quote one. A pressure gauge on an outdoor hose bib gives you your real number in a minute.

If the reading looks high, a pressure regulator near the main valve is the usual fix. High pressure wears out fixtures, hoses and water heaters faster, which is why a leak that seems random can have a steady cause. Low pressure, on the other hand, may point to a leak or a corroded line, covered on our water line repair page.

Strapping the water heater

California law says new and replacement water heaters, and existing residential water heaters, must be braced, anchored or strapped to resist falling or horizontal displacement from earthquake motion. Union City has adopted the 2025 California codes, including the plumbing code. That applies to a water heater on a hillside lot as much as anywhere else.

When a tank is replaced, ask who straps it and whether a permit is involved. The city says a permit is needed for most construction, and the Building Division at 510.675.5313 can answer for a specific job. Our water heater replacement page explains the steps.

Source type: California Health and Safety Code section 19211

Before you call about a backup

1

Stop adding water

Hold off on laundry, dishes and showers. Every drain you use adds to the backup.

2

Note which fixtures are affected

The lowest fixture in the house usually shows trouble first.

3

Call USD about the main

USD answers at (510) 477-7500. If its main is clear, the blockage is probably in your lateral.

4

Locate the cleanout

It sits just outside the building or behind the sidewalk. Older homes may not have one.
