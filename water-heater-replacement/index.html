Urgent situation

Your water heater has failed and water is on the floor

A tank water heater fails in a predictable way. The steel liner corrodes, a pinhole opens, and water seeps from the base or the seams. By the time you see a puddle, the tank is finished. Repair is not an option for a leaking tank, because no one can patch the inside of a sealed vessel.

Same-day replacement is often possible when the right size unit is on the truck or at the supply house, and after-hours calls get the same attention as daytime ones. The first job on arrival is to stop the water and make the area safe, then confirm the fuel type, the tank size and the venting before anything is ordered.

If you smell gas at any point, do not stay to troubleshoot. Leave the building, call 911, then call PG&E at 1-800-743-5000. Tell us afterward and we will plan the replacement around the gas company's all-clear.

Choosing a fuel

Gas, electric or hybrid heat pump: what changes at replacement

Gas tank

The familiar choice with fast recovery. It needs a proper flue, combustion air, a gas shutoff, a sediment trap and a flexible connector. Venting that no longer fits the new unit is the most common surprise.

Electric tank

Simple venting and no gas line, but it needs a dedicated circuit sized for the unit. Recovery is slower than gas, so household hot water habits matter more.

Hybrid heat pump

Uses a heat pump on top of the tank to move heat instead of making it. It needs a dedicated circuit, a condensate drain, and open air around the unit because it cools the space it sits in.

Same size or a change

Swapping like for like keeps the existing gas, venting and electrical. Changing fuel means new work in the wall or panel, and that is where permits and inspections come in.

Bay Area rules

BAAQMD Rule 9-6 and the heat pump incentive in Union City

The Bay Area Air Quality Management District's Rule 9-6 sets a zero-NOx standard for new natural gas water heaters. It is currently scheduled to take effect in January 2027, and a proposed one-year delay is under Board consideration. The rule applies when a burned-out unit is replaced, so a heater that dies today is replaced under the rules in force on the day of the swap.

The practical payoff is timing. If you are choosing between gas and a heat pump, ask which gas models are available on your install date.

BayREN pays a 1,000 dollar incentive to the participating contractor for a qualifying heat pump unit (UEF 3.3 or higher) that replaces a gas heater, for Ava Community Energy customers. Union City is served by Ava, and customers may return to PG&E at any time, so check which one is on your bill before counting on it.

What a proper replacement visit covers

  • ✓Shutdown and drain. Water, fuel and power are isolated and the old tank is drained safely.
  • ✓Seismic bracing. California Health and Safety Code 19211 requires new and replacement water heaters to be braced, anchored or strapped against earthquake motion.
  • ✓Code connections. New flexible connectors, a relief valve with a proper discharge line, a gas shutoff and sediment trap on gas units, and a pan or drain where the location calls for it.
  • ✓Venting and combustion air. Gas flues are checked for draft and condition before the burner is lit.

Size and sizing

Sizing the new tank without guessing

Tank size follows the number of people, bathrooms and fixtures that run at once. A tank that was fine for two adults often struggles once a household grows. The first-hour rating on the label matters more than the gallon count.

If your family has changed, tell the plumber. If the old unit never kept up, a larger tank or a different fuel may fix a problem the last installer built in. For very high demand, tankless water heaters are worth comparing.
