What the Camera Report Shows

Pipe material and condition

Clay, cast iron, plastic and fiber pipe each fail in different ways. The camera shows which you have and how it is holding up.

Roots

We see whether roots are entering at joints and how dense they are, which tells us whether cleaning or repair is the realistic path.

Cracks, offsets and breaks

A joint that has shifted or a pipe that has split is clear on camera, along with how far the damage runs.

Bellies and standing water

A low spot holds water. If the camera shows a pool where the pipe should be dry, the grade is off.

Location and depth

A locator on the camera head marks the pipe's position above ground, so a repair can be planned around driveways and trees.

Video you can keep

The footage is useful for repair planning, for permit steps and for talking to a seller or another contractor.

Camera Footage and Local Sewer Rules

USD says an old capped lateral can only be reused if it is connected, in good condition and the video is submitted, and it also notes a backflow device may be prudent in hilly areas. A USD repair permit is required before any lateral work.

So inspection footage is not just curiosity. It is the record that supports the repair permit, and it shows whether an existing lateral can be reused in a remodel or rebuild.

Buying a Home? Check the Sewer First

A general home inspection rarely looks inside the sewer lateral. In Union City the owner is responsible for the whole lateral to the main, so once you close, a hidden break is your repair bill. A camera visit before you commit costs far less than a surprise afterwards.

Share the footage with your agent so any issue can be addressed in negotiation, and ask the seller for any past plumbing records. We can also advise whether the finding is minor or something to price into the offer.

If we find a problem, see sewer line repair for the options. Serving Union City and nearby areas, including Fremont.

What an Inspection Does Not Tell You

A camera sees the inside surface only. It does not measure soil movement or predict when a sound pipe will fail. If the line is full of debris, we clear it first, see hydro jetting, and then re-run the camera so the picture is accurate.
